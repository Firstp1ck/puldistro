Privacy

Privacy-friendly by design.

Puldistro is a static website. The quiz runs in your browser and does not require an account.

Data collection

  • No quiz answers are sent to a server by this site.
  • No cookies are required for the quiz or glossary.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are included in these files.

External links

The site links to third-party projects such as Linux distributions, app websites, documentation pages, and compatibility databases. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Local storage

The site stores only your light/dark theme preference in local storage so the choice persists across pages. Quiz answers are not stored; refreshing or restarting the quiz resets the answers.
