About this project

A practical helper for choosing Linux.

Puldistro helps newcomers compare Linux distributions and common app alternatives without needing Linux background knowledge.

What it does

  • Asks plain-language questions about your computer, habits, apps, games, and maintenance comfort.
  • Recommends Linux distributions and desktop interfaces based on your preferences.
  • Highlights common migration blockers such as Windows-only apps, anti-cheat games, and vendor hardware utilities.

What it does not do

It cannot test your exact computer or guarantee every device, game, or professional app will work. Before replacing Windows, test your real workflow with a live USB, separate drive, dual boot, or spare machine.
